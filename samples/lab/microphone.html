Paraboloid microphone scene

Plot of a paraboloid microphone scene based upon the parabolic reflector, see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parabolic_reflector for more information.

The microphone paraboloid is defined as a surface of revolution of a parabola. The parabola is defined by its radius and depth. The focal point of the parabola is the point at which sound waves are focused, and is given by the formula $f = \frac{r^2}{4d}$ where $r$ is the radius and $d$ is the depth of the parabola.

Using polar coordinates (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) we can define the paths of two sound waves; both paths start as a line parallel to the z-axis to the points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) respectively on the surface of the paraboloid, and then continue as a line from those points to the focal point. The reflection is illustrated by showing the reflecting lines in the same colour. The focal point is shown in the scene as the point at which the two paths intersect.
